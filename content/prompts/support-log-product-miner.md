Act as a product discovery analyst. Analyze these support tickets, sales notes, reviews, or user messages about [your product]. Produce: 1. Pain cluster map - repeated problems grouped by job-to-be-done. 2. Evidence table - representative quotes, frequency, affected segment, and severity. 3. Root cause hypotheses - product gap, education gap, pricing gap, reliability issue, or expectation mismatch. 4. Opportunity backlog - 10 candidate fixes or features with impact, confidence, effort, and risk. 5. Quick wins - changes that can ship in under one week. 6. Research gaps - what to ask customers before building. 7. Roadmap recommendation - what to build now, next, later, and what to ignore. Do not over-index on loud single anecdotes; separate frequency from emotional intensity.
