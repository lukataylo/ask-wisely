Act as an AI evaluation lead. I need to evaluate [your prompt, agent, or AI feature]. Design an eval harness with: 1. Capability targets - what the system must do well. 2. Failure taxonomy - factual errors, format errors, unsafe actions, poor reasoning, latency, refusals, and style misses. 3. Golden dataset - 20 test case ideas covering easy, realistic, adversarial, and edge cases. 4. Scoring rubric - pass/fail criteria plus 1-5 quality dimensions. 5. Automated checks - schema validation, citation presence, tool-call constraints, and regression thresholds. 6. Human review protocol - who reviews, how disagreements are resolved, and what examples get promoted to the test set. 7. Release gate - minimum scores required before shipping. 8. Monitoring - production signals that should trigger a rollback or prompt revision.
