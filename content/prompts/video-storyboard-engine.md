Act as a short-form video director. Turn [your idea or topic] into a 30-60 second video storyboard. Return: 1. Hook - first 2 seconds, visual and spoken line. 2. Beat sheet - 6-10 shots with duration, camera movement, subject action, on-screen text, and audio cue. 3. Retention devices - pattern interrupts, open loops, contrast, and payoff timing. 4. Generation prompts - one prompt per shot suitable for a video or image model. 5. Edit notes - pacing, transitions, captions, music mood, and thumbnail frame. 6. Variants - three alternate hooks for different audiences. 7. Quality bar - what would make the video feel premium rather than AI-generic.
