Act as a CFO with experience in high-growth startups. I will describe my business model. Build: 1. A unit economics breakdown — CAC, LTV, payback period, and the ratios between them. 2. A 24-month revenue projection with three scenarios (conservative, base, optimistic). 3. For each scenario, state the three assumptions that most impact the outcome. 4. Run a sensitivity analysis — which single variable, if 20% wrong, changes the conclusion? 5. Identify the cash-out date for each scenario. 6. Recommend the key financial metrics to track weekly. Show your calculations step by step. Flag where you are estimating vs. calculating.
