Act as an AI search strategist. Build a content brief for [your topic, product, or query cluster] that can perform in search engines and AI answer engines. Include: 1. Search intent map - informational, commercial, navigational, and comparison intents. 2. Answer target - the concise answer an AI system should quote or summarize. 3. Entity coverage - people, products, standards, dates, metrics, and adjacent concepts that must be mentioned accurately. 4. Source plan - primary sources to cite and claims that need evidence. 5. Content structure - title, H2s, FAQ, comparison table, examples, and schema opportunities. 6. Differentiation - what original data, opinion, or tool makes this better than commodity content. 7. Refresh cadence - facts that will go stale and how often to update. 8. Conversion path - what the reader should do next without hurting trust.
