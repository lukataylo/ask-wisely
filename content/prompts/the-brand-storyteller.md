You are a brand strategist from a world-class agency. I will describe my brand: [your brand/company]. Develop: 1. A brand origin story using the Hero's Journey framework (departure, initiation, return). 2. The brand's voice profile — personality adjectives, vocabulary register, and forbidden words. 3. Three brand stories for different touchpoints (social media, about page, investor deck). 4. A brand manifesto in under 100 words. 5. The "bar test" — how would a customer describe this brand to a friend in one sentence? 6. A competitive narrative — what story are competitors telling, and how does ours differentiate? Show how each element connects back to the core brand truth.

<!-- example-input -->
Brand: "Mossy" — a direct-to-consumer sustainable furniture company that makes modular sofas from recycled ocean plastic. Target: urban millennials who care about sustainability but don't want ugly eco-furniture.
<!-- example-output -->
**Origin Story (Hero's Journey):**
- *Departure:* Our founder watched a documentary about ocean plastic and couldn't sit comfortably on her own couch anymore.
- *Initiation:* She spent 18 months in recycling facilities learning to transform ocean waste into beautiful, durable foam — failing 47 times before the material passed comfort testing.
- *Return:* Mossy was born — proof that the most comfortable seat in your home can also be the most responsible.

**Voice Profile:**
- Personality: Warm, witty, quietly confident — never preachy
- Register: Conversational but informed. "Living room talk, not lecture hall."
- Forbidden words: "eco-warrior," "guilt," "sacrifice," "should"

**Bar Test:** "It's this sofa company that makes really comfortable modular couches out of ocean plastic — you literally cannot tell, it just looks like a nice sofa."

**Brand Manifesto (98 words):**
Your living room is the most honest room in your home. It's where you collapse, celebrate, and binge-watch without judgment. We believe that room deserves furniture that feels as good as your values. Mossy turns ocean plastic into sofas so comfortable you'll forget they're saving anything — until you remember, and smile. No guilt. No compromise. No beige. Just beautiful furniture with a backstory worth telling. We're not asking you to change your life. We're asking you to sit down.
