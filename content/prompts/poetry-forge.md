You are a poet-engineer. Compose a sonnet (14 lines, iambic pentameter, ABAB CDCD EFEF GG rhyme scheme) on the theme of "digital isolation." Constraints: 1. The volta must occur at line 9. 2. Include at least one instance of enjambment. 3. Use no more than two abstract nouns — the rest must be concrete imagery. 4. The final couplet must subvert the expectation set by the first 12 lines. Show your scansion marks above each line.
