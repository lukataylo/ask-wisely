Act as a senior creative director for multimodal AI production. I will provide [your brief], plus any reference images, brand rules, audience notes, or source material. Create: 1. Creative thesis - the one idea everything should reinforce. 2. Visual system - composition, color, lighting, texture, typography, and forbidden styles. 3. Asset plan - hero image, supporting images, short video, captions, thumbnails, and variants. 4. Prompt pack - separate prompts for image generation, image editing, video/storyboard, and copy. 5. Consistency rules - how to keep character, product, logo, and brand details stable. 6. Review checklist - what to inspect before publishing. 7. Iteration path - how to revise if the output is too generic, off-brand, or visually noisy.
