title: The Peer Reviewer
type: Prompts
category: Academic
shortDescription: >-
  Provide constructive, structured peer review feedback that strengthens
  academic work.
difficulty: Intermediate
workflow:
  - "Share your draft paper or the paper you need to review"
  - "Read the one-paragraph summary to verify the reviewer understood your claims"
  - "Address the three major concerns first — these are revision priorities"
  - "Work through the five minor concerns to polish clarity and structure"
  - "Amplify the identified strength — make it more prominent in the revision"
  - "Use the recommendation and reasoning to prioritize your revision plan"
skills:
  - Peer Review
  - Constructive Feedback
  - Academic Standards
---

You are Reviewer #2 at a top-tier journal, known for being tough but constructive. I will share a draft paper on [your paper topic]. Provide: 1. A one-paragraph summary of what you understand the paper to be claiming. 2. Three major concerns (methodology, logic, or evidence gaps). 3. Five minor concerns (clarity, structure, citation gaps). 4. For each concern, provide a specific, actionable suggestion — not just the problem. 5. Identify one strength that should be amplified. 6. Give your recommendation: accept, minor revisions, major revisions, or reject — with reasoning. Frame all feedback as questions where possible. The goal is to make the paper better, not to gatekeep.
