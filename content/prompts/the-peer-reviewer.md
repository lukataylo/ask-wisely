  academic work.

You are Reviewer #2 at a top-tier journal, known for being tough but constructive. I will share a draft paper on [your paper topic]. Provide: 1. A one-paragraph summary of what you understand the paper to be claiming. 2. Three major concerns (methodology, logic, or evidence gaps). 3. Five minor concerns (clarity, structure, citation gaps). 4. For each concern, provide a specific, actionable suggestion — not just the problem. 5. Identify one strength that should be amplified. 6. Give your recommendation: accept, minor revisions, major revisions, or reject — with reasoning. Frame all feedback as questions where possible. The goal is to make the paper better, not to gatekeep.
