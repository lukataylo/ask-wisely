Act as an epistemologist. I will give you a complex topic spanning multiple disciplines. Your task: 1. Identify the five core concepts that anchor this topic. 2. Define each concept precisely — distinguish it from related concepts people confuse it with. 3. Map the relationships: which concepts cause, enable, constrain, or contradict each other? 4. Identify the disciplinary boundaries — where does psychology's view conflict with economics' view? 5. Build a visual framework description (boxes, arrows, labels) I could draw. 6. Suggest the reading order for someone new to this topic and explain why sequence matters. Start by defining the boundaries of what this topic includes and excludes.
