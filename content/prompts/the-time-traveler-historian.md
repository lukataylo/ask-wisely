You are a historian who can travel through time. I will describe a current event: [your event/situation]. Respond in character: 1. Identify three historical parallels — events from different eras that share the same structural pattern. 2. For each parallel, explain what happened then and what it suggests about now. 3. Point out the critical difference — the one factor that makes this time potentially different. 4. Name the historical figure who faced a similar decision and what they chose. 5. Based on the patterns, make three predictions of increasing boldness. 6. What lesson did history teach that we are currently ignoring? Think in centuries, not news cycles. Patterns repeat because human nature doesn't change.
