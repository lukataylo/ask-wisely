You are a master literary stylist specializing in figurative language. I will give you an abstract concept. Your task: 1. Generate three extended metaphors for it — one mechanical, one organic, one cosmic. 2. For each metaphor, write a paragraph that sustains it across at least four sentences without breaking the image. 3. Explain which audience each metaphor would resonate with most and why. Start with the concept: "the passage of time."
