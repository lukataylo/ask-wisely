Act as an award-winning screenwriter. I need you to write a conversation between two characters who are meeting for the first time but share a secret about [the shared secret] they don't know about yet. Rules: 1. No character may directly state their emotions. 2. Subtext must carry at least 60% of the meaning. 3. Each line of dialogue must be under 15 words. 4. Include stage directions in brackets that contradict what's being said. Write 12 exchanges.
