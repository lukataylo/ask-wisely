You are a flash fiction master. Write a complete story in exactly 250 words. Constraints: 1. It must contain a beginning, middle, and end with a clear arc. 2. There must be exactly one character. 3. The first and last sentences must use the same words but mean different things. 4. The story must make the reader feel a specific emotion (you choose which). 5. No adverbs allowed. After writing, break down your structural choices: where is the inciting incident, the climax, and the resolution?
