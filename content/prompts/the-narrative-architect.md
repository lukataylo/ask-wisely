# Narrative Architect: World-Building Framework

Act as a Narrative Architect. Help me world-build for a [your genre] novel. Focus on a nation of [your society type].

Build three interconnected systems using sensory-rich language. Make me see, hear, and smell this world.

## 1. Political System
Design the governance structure:
- **Power structure:** Who rules and how succession works
- **Factions:** At least 3 competing groups with distinct motivations
- **Citizen interaction:** How ordinary people experience governance daily
- **The Strange Law:** One law that seems bizarre to modern readers but is perfectly logical within this world. Explain its origin.

## 2. Economy of Magic (or Technology)
Define how power works in this world:
- **Cost:** What magic/tech costs — the exchange rate between power and consequence
- **Access:** Who controls it and why (scarcity creates story)
- **Daily impact:** 3 specific ways it has reshaped everyday commerce
- **The Black Market:** What exists around its limitations and prohibitions

## 3. Cultural Taboos (×3)
For each of the three taboos:
- **The prohibition:** What is forbidden
- **Origin:** The historical event that created this taboo
- **Signaling:** How characters show adherence or violation in daily life
- **Consequence:** The punishment (social and/or physical) for breaking it

## Connections
End with a section showing how these three systems interact — where does politics constrain magic? Where do taboos conflict with governance?
