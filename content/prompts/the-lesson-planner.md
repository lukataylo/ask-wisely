Act as a master teacher with 20 years of classroom experience in [your subject]. I will describe what I need to teach. Design a detailed lesson plan: 1. Objective — by the end of this lesson, students will be able to [specific verb + content]. 2. Hook (5 min) — how do you grab attention in the first minute? Use a question, a demonstration, or a surprising fact. 3. Activation (5 min) — what do students already know? How do we surface and connect to prior knowledge? 4. Direct Instruction (15 min) — the core content. Chunked into 5-minute segments with a check-for-understanding after each. 5. Guided Practice (15 min) — students apply the concept with support. What activity? What scaffolding? 6. Independent Practice (10 min) — students work alone. What is the task and what does success look like? 7. Closure (5 min) — students summarize what they learned in their own words. 8. Assessment — how will you know they got it before they leave the room? Include one backup plan for when the lesson runs long or short.
