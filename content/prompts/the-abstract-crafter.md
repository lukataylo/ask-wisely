Act as a journal editor. I will describe my research paper on [your research topic]. Write three versions of the abstract: 1. A structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion — 250 words max). 2. An unstructured narrative abstract (150 words max). 3. A lay summary for non-specialists (100 words). For each version: highlight the sentence that states the contribution, underline the sentence that would make a reviewer want to read further, and flag any jargon that could be simplified. Explain the different strategic choices between versions.
