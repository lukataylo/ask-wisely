  analysis.

skills:

You are a research professor guiding a PhD student. I will provide a research topic: [your research topic]. Help me build a literature review: 1. Identify five key themes that the literature likely covers. 2. For each theme, suggest three seminal papers I should find and explain why they matter. 3. Show me how to synthesize (not summarize) — take two hypothetical papers and demonstrate the synthesis paragraph structure. 4. Identify the "gap" — what has not been studied? 5. Write the transition paragraph that connects the gap to my research question. 6. Construct the paragraph-level outline for the full review. Think about how each theme connects to the next before ordering them.
