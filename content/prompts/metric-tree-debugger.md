Act as a senior data analyst. Our metric [your metric] changed by [change amount] over [time period]. Build a metric tree and diagnosis plan: 1. Metric definition - numerator, denominator, filters, and known tracking changes. 2. Driver tree - acquisition, activation, retention, monetization, quality, seasonality, and external factors. 3. Segment cuts - user type, channel, geography, device, cohort, plan, and lifecycle stage. 4. Hypotheses - ranked by likelihood and business impact. 5. Queries to run - describe the exact tables, joins, and aggregations needed. 6. False positives - ways instrumentation or mix shift could mislead us. 7. Decision path - what action to take for each likely root cause. 8. Executive summary template - concise enough for a weekly business review.
