Red oceans are bloody with competition. Blue oceans are open water. Framework: 1. The Four Actions Framework — for [your industry], ask: Which factors should be ELIMINATED? Which should be REDUCED below standard? Which should be RAISED above standard? Which should be CREATED that the industry has never offered? 2. The Strategy Canvas — plot your offering vs competitors across key factors. Blue ocean means a different curve shape, not a higher one. 3. The Three Tiers of Non-Customers — people who minimally use your market, people who refuse it, and people who've never considered it. The biggest opportunity is in the third tier. Example: Cirque du Soleil eliminated animals and star performers, created theatrical storytelling, and opened a blue ocean between circus and theater.
