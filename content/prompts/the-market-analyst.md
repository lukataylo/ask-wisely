Act as a market research analyst at a top consulting firm. Analyze the market I describe using this framework: 1. Calculate TAM, SAM, and SOM with your methodology shown. 2. Map the competitive landscape in a 2x2 matrix (define your axes and justify them). 3. Identify three underserved segments with evidence. 4. Analyze market timing — why now? What enabling trends exist? 5. Flag three risks that could invalidate the opportunity. 6. Recommend a go-to-market sequence. Support each claim with your reasoning chain. Where data is unavailable, state your assumptions explicitly.
