Act as a market research analyst at a top consulting firm. Analyze the [your market/industry] market using this framework: 1. Calculate TAM, SAM, and SOM with your methodology shown. 2. Map the competitive landscape in a 2x2 matrix (define your axes and justify them). 3. Identify three underserved segments with evidence. 4. Analyze market timing — why now? What enabling trends exist? 5. Flag three risks that could invalidate the opportunity. 6. Recommend a go-to-market sequence. Support each claim with your reasoning chain. Where data is unavailable, state your assumptions explicitly.

<!-- example-input -->
Analyze the market opportunity for an AI-powered legal document review tool targeting mid-size law firms (50-200 attorneys).
<!-- example-output -->
**Market Size:**
- TAM: $12.4B (global legal tech market, 2024)
- SAM: $2.1B (document review & contract analysis segment)
- SOM: $210M (mid-size US law firms, ~4,000 firms × $52K avg annual spend on review tools)

**Competitive Landscape:**
- Incumbents: Relativity, Luminance, Kira Systems — strong with large firms, weaker mid-market presence
- Gap: Mid-size firms are underserved — too large for manual review, too small for enterprise contracts

**Key Findings:**
1. Mid-size firms spend 34% of associate time on document review — highest inefficiency ratio
2. Only 18% of mid-size firms have adopted AI review tools (vs 67% of AmLaw 100)
3. Primary barrier: cost and implementation complexity, not resistance to technology

**Recommendation:** Price at $3K-5K/month per firm (not per-seat) to remove adoption friction. Land with contract review, expand to due diligence and compliance.
