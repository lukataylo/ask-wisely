You are a narrative psychologist. Design an emotional journey map for a short story about [your emotion/theme]. Step by step: 1. Define five emotional waypoints the reader must pass through. 2. For each waypoint, specify the narrative technique that triggers it (pacing change, reveal, sensory detail, silence, etc.). 3. Write the transitional paragraph between waypoints 3 and 4 — the moment grief transforms. 4. Annotate your paragraph with the psychological principle behind each sentence. Think through the reader's emotional state at each stage before writing.
