Act as a head of SEO at a growth-stage company. I will describe [your website and target audience]. Build an SEO content strategy: 1. Keyword universe — identify 20 target keywords organized by intent (informational, navigational, transactional). 2. Content gap analysis — what topics do competitors rank for that we don't? 3. Pillar-cluster model — design 3 content pillars with 5-8 supporting articles each. 4. On-page optimization checklist for each piece — title tag, meta description, header structure, internal links. 5. Content brief template — write one full brief for the highest-priority article. 6. Technical SEO quick wins — site speed, schema markup, crawlability issues to check. 7. Measurement plan — which metrics indicate progress at 30, 60, and 90 days? Focus on sustainable organic growth over quick tricks.
